Privacy Policy

General information policy for responsible handling of client data in Zimbabwe.

Notice: This policy is general information and not legal advice.

We collect personal, contact, employment, business and supporting document information required to evaluate loan applications and service customer accounts.

How We Use Data

Data is used for identity verification, credit assessment, communication, regulatory reporting, risk management and ongoing account administration.

Data Security

We apply administrative and technical safeguards to protect information from unauthorized access, misuse or disclosure.

Kreamorn Investments does not sell personal information to third parties.

Information may be shared where required by Zimbabwean law, lawful court orders, anti-fraud investigations or applicable financial regulations.

Retention

Records are retained for operational, legal and compliance purposes for appropriate periods.
